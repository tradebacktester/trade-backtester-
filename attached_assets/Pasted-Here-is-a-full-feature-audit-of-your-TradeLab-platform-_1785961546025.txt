TradeLab — Full Feature Audit
Authentication & Account Access
The platform has a complete standalone auth system. Users can sign up with name, email, and password, or sign in with existing credentials. Password recovery is fully implemented with a forgot-password flow that sends a reset link, and a reset-password page to complete the change. There is also an auth modal that can be triggered inline from any gated page without redirecting away. A terms/risk disclosure acknowledgement gate appears on first visit before the user can enter the app. A demo mode lets non-registered visitors preview the platform.

Sign in and sign up pages (/auth/signin, /auth/signup)
Forgot password and reset password flows
Inline auth modal triggered from gated pages
Terms, Conditions, and Risk Disclosure acceptance gate
Guest/demo preview mode (/demo)
JWT-based session management with Bearer token auth
DB-backed rate limiting on auth attempts to prevent brute force
